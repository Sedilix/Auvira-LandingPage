AUVIRA LOGO PACK

The mark: four gold strands, one for each phase of the cycle, turning clockwise
and curling back in - always moving, always returning.

FOLDERS
mark/      the swirl on its own
lockup/    swirl + "Auvira" wordmark (horizontal and stacked)
app-icon/  app icons (1024/512/180 px) and favicon (32/192/512 px + SVG)

VERSIONS
glow       primary logo: gold swirl in the sunset aura. App, website, slides, social.
gold       flat #745022 (Auvira gold) - the main flat colour. Merch, embroidery, foil, print.
deep-gold  flat #7A4E17. Alternative for documents and small sizes.
white      for dark backgrounds and photos.
black      for black-only print, stamps and forms.

FILE TYPES
SVG  vector - use for print, merch, embroidery, signage (scales to any size)
PDF  vector - send to printers and embroiderers
PNG  2048 px, transparent background - use in slides, docs, social

RULES
- Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the "A" in Auvira.
- Minimum size: mark 24 px on screen / 8 mm in print; embroidery at least 2.5 cm wide.
- Don't recolour, stretch, rotate, add shadows or outlines.
- Use the glow version on light champagne or white backgrounds.
- Wordmark: Cormorant Garamond SemiBold Italic, converted to outlines (no font needed).

Auvira and PMOS, explained

Auvira helps women in Singapore make sense of hormonal symptoms, starting with PMOS (formerly known as PCOS).

Auvira is a Singapore-based hormonal health platform for women. It connects your cycle, symptoms and wearable data with an AI health companion and a coordinated care team, so you get clear insights and a single plan instead of scattered advice. Our first specialist programme is for PMOS.

Yes. PMOS stands for polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome. It is the new name for polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), announced in May 2026. The new name reflects that the condition affects hormones and metabolism, not just the ovaries. The diagnostic criteria have not changed.

No. Auvira does not diagnose conditions or replace your doctor. It gives you insights and a starting point, and helps you reach the right specialist. Diagnoses and treatment plans come from licensed partner physicians in Singapore.

Auvira is for any woman navigating hormonal change, whether you already have a diagnosis or just feel something isn't right. Common signs include irregular or missed periods, jawline acne, unwanted hair growth, unexplained weight changes and ongoing fatigue.

Your care team brings together a lead physician, a care coordinator who is your single point of contact, a dietitian who understands Asian and hawker diets, and a cycle-aware movement coach. They work from one shared plan, supported day to day by the Auvira AI companion.

With your permission, Auvira reads biometrics such as sleep and resting heart rate from wearables like Garmin. It lines them up with your cycle phase and logged symptoms to spot patterns worth discussing with your care team.

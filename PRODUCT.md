# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

HTML5, Vanilla CSS, Vanilla JavaScript, Vite (bundled with zero external runtime dependencies)

## Users

Primary: Women in Singapore aged 20 to 35 experiencing unexplained hormonal symptoms (irregular or missed cycles, cystic jawline acne, hirsutism, unexpected weight changes, fatigue).
Secondary: Singapore General Practitioners, Gynaecologists, Endocrinologists, Dietitians, and Clinic Directors interested in integrated PMOS care pathways.

## Product Purpose

Auvira (*Aura* [light] + *Veritas* [truth]) is a Singapore-based, cycle-aware hormonal wellbeing platform for women. It should feel calm, grown-up, and trustworthy—never clinical or girly. It transitions patients from years of dismissed symptoms and conflicting advice to verified answers, daily cycle insights, and an integrated care plan in weeks.

## Positioning

Unlike generic symptom trackers or unregulated supplement sellers, Auvira provides:
1. Her longitudinal hormone record synthesized across cycles, symptoms, Asian-calibrated lab panels, and Garmin wearables.
2. Direct navigation to PMOS-trained doctors in Singapore with guideline-based testing ordered upfront and clinical briefs prepared in advance.
3. A synchronized care team (lead physician, care coordinator, dietitian for Asian diets, cycle movement coach, and daily AI companion) working under one unified 90-day plan.

## Operating Context

- Singapore healthcare landscape: Private GPs, KKH IVF clinic, SingHealth, and private specialist clinics.
- Nutrition calibrated for local Southeast Asian diets and hawker dining realities.
- Biometric tracking with Garmin Health API integration.
- Singapore spelling (British English: colour, personalise, specialised, programme) and SGD references.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities:
  - Free 10-Minute Asian-Calibrated Hormone Screener generating personalized risk assessment and blood test checklists.
  - Native display of phone screens on pearl cards with soft aura glow.
  - Single point-of-contact care coordinator workflow.
  - Pilot cohort enrolment for 30–50 Singapore women.
  - Medical partner onboarding for Singapore physicians.
- Mandatory Copy Rules (From Brand & UI Style Guide):
  - **Never say Auvira diagnoses anything.** Say it gives answers, insights or a starting point, and helps her find the right specialist.
  - **Auvira never replaces her doctor.**
  - **Sentence case** for headings and buttons ("Start your check-in", not "Start Your Check-In").
  - Short sentences. One idea per line on the landing page.
